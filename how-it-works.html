INQ / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

How INQ works.
The token system.

INQ eliminates physical queues on campus by replacing lines with live virtual tokens. Instead of staring at people's backs, you join remotely, receive an active token, track your live progress, and walk up only when it's your turn.

The 3-Step Lifecycle

STEP 01

Select & Join

Choose your campus service (Dean Office, Printing Shop, or Café) and specify your exact need online from any phone or laptop.

STEP 02

Get Virtual Token

Your token is instantly generated with a unique sequence number and a dynamic wait estimate based on active service speed.

STEP 03

Arrive on Turn

Go study, grab lunch, or relax. Check your live counter, and reach the service right when your token is ready.

How Tokens Work Across Services

Each campus location has its own customized queue workflow tailored to its operational speed:

DEAN OFFICE QUEUE D-27

Sequential verification: Designed for signatures, document verification, and collections. Tokens progress in strict order with an average of ~3 minutes per student.

PRINTING SHOP QUEUE P-34

Job-complexity calculation: Upload your file in advance. The token estimates readiness by calculating total pages, color vs B&W, and sides.

CAMPUS CAFÉ QUEUE C-18

Kitchen prep window: Order snacks and beverages ahead. Kitchen staff process orders concurrently and tokens notify you when food is boxed and hot.

Real-Time Dynamic Wait Times

INQ doesn't show fixed guesses. The system calculates live waiting times using real factors:

  • Active students currently ahead in your specific service line
  • Dynamic crowd levels (Low, Moderate, and High crowd status)
  • Historical average completion time for your selected request type
  • Concurrent processing capacity at peak campus rush hours
